FAQ

Common questions, answered.

Is Nove free to use?
Yes. All commands and features are available to every server, with no paid tier or subscription.
How do I change the bot prefix?
Use ,setprefix [new prefix]. The default is a comma, and each server can set its own.
What permissions does Nove need?
Nove requests Administrator so every feature works. Keep its role above others in your hierarchy for moderation to function correctly.
How do I use the Embed Builder?
Fill in the fields on the Embed page, watch the Discord preview update live, then copy the generated command and paste it into any channel Nove is in.
Where do I get help or report a bug?
Contact the developer through the links on the developer page.
